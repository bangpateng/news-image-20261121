A newspaper front page summing up the world's news of Saturday 21 November
2026, built for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261121)
bounty "Sum up 21 November's world news in one image".

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, no network, fully deterministic)
draws the front page and writes it to `/output/entry.png` (1024×1024,
byte-identical on every run). Every letter is drawn from a small 5×7 bitmap
font, and the PNG bytes are encoded by hand.

Lead story: Russian missiles crash in Poland (reported 21 November). Also
covered: the overnight attack on Ukraine, the foiled Denmark assassination
plot, Estonia closing its border crossings, the Siberia An-12 crash, Cyclone
Akvan in Odisha, the Wall Street sell-off, and the US approval of nearly
880,000 AI chip exports to China.
